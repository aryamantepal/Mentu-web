Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short

Mentu reads the names of buttons on your screen so it can show you where to tap. It sends those names to Mentu's server to work out the next step. It never sends a picture of your screen.

What Mentu reads

While you use Mentu, it reads four things from your phone.

  • The names of the buttons on your screen.
  • The headings on your screen.
  • Which app is open.
  • What you asked Mentu to help with.

What leaves your phone

At every step, the four things above go to Mentu's server. The server passes them on to Mentu's decision service, TypeSafe, which works out the next step.

Mentu's server does not store them beyond the request.

To be confirmed. How long TypeSafe keeps requests, whether it uses them to train its models, and whether it signs a data processing agreement. These are not known yet.

What Mentu never reads or sends

  • Passwords and PINs.
  • Payment screens.
  • The lock screen.
  • Your photos.
  • The contents of your messages.

What Mentu stores

If you sign in with Google, Mentu stores:

  • Your Google account ID.
  • Your settings: your name, text size, language and chosen apps.
  • Your sessions and usage counts.

If you use Mentu as a guest, your data is deleted when the guest session expires.

To be confirmed. How long Mentu keeps account data while the account is open.

Who helps Mentu run

These companies handle data for Mentu.

  • Google Cloud. Part of how Mentu's server and Google sign-in run.
  • Supabase. Where account data is kept.
  • TypeSafe. Mentu's decision service. It receives what leaves your phone, as described above.
  • Your phone's speech recogniser. It turns your voice into words. It is part of your phone, and your phone's maker decides how it works.
  • Vercel. It hosts this website. Like any host, it sees the request your browser makes to load a page.

To be confirmed. Which Google Cloud services Mentu uses, and what each company's role is in full. Check these against the app before launch.

This website has no analytics today. It sets no cookies.

  • Children. Mentu is not made for children under 13.
