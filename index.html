What is Mentu?

Mentu is a free Android app that helps people who find phones hard. You say what you want to do, like "send a photo on WhatsApp", and Mentu highlights where to tap on your own screen, one step at a time. It teaches; it never taps for you.

How it works

  1. Say what you want

    Tell Mentu what you want to do, in your own words.

  2. Look for the gold ring

    The ring circles the button to tap next.

  3. Tap it yourself

    Mentu waits for your tap, then shows you the next step.

Our promise

  • Mentu teaches. It never taps for you.
  • It never reads your passwords or PINs.
  • It never works on payment screens.
  • Mentu never keeps what's on your screen.

Helping a parent with their phone?

Set Mentu up for them once. After that, they can ask Mentu instead of waiting for you to call back.
