How it works

  1. Say what you want

    Tell Mentu what you want to do, in your own words.

  2. Look for the gold ring

    The ring circles the button to tap next.

  3. Tap it yourself

    Mentu waits for your tap, then shows you the next step.

Helping a parent with their phone?

Set Mentu up for them once. It takes about 5 minutes. You turn on one switch in Settings, and Mentu shows you where it is.

After that, they can ask Mentu instead of waiting for you to call back.

Things you can say

  • "Send a photo on WhatsApp"
  • "Video call on WhatsApp"
  • "Make text bigger"
  • "Turn on Wi-Fi"

Our promise

To show you where to tap, Mentu reads the names of the buttons on your screen. It sends them to Mentu's server to work out the next step. It never sends a picture of your screen.

  • Mentu teaches. It never taps for you.
  • It never reads your passwords or PINs.
  • It never works on payment screens.
  • Mentu never keeps what's on your screen.
