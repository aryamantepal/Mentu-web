Delete your account

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Two ways to delete

1. In the app

This is the fastest way. In Mentu, tap Settings. Tap Your account. Then tap Delete my account.

2. On this page

Deleting on the website is being set up. It is not ready yet.

Until then, delete your account in the app. If you cannot, ask Mentu's support to delete it for you.

To be confirmed. Support email: to be added when Mentu has its domain. How fast Mentu answers is also to be confirmed.

What is deleted

  • Your account.
  • Your settings.
  • Your sessions.
  • Your usage counts.

What is not deleted

Mentu keeps anonymous counts that have no link to you. They cannot be traced back to you.

Paid plans

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play. Mentu itself is free.
